Zero data. No servers.

Quakpit has no backend and no database. Your calendar is read directly on your device, kept only in memory, and never written to disk or sent anywhere. No telemetry, no analytics. The only thing it can store is your encrypted sign-in token — and only if you ask it to.

▸ in case you wondered

Questions & answers

Which calendars work?

Add a calendar by iCal/ICS link (the easiest — no login) or connect Apple iCloud. Google and Outlook are on the way. Everything is read-only; the free plan connects one source, Pro combines several at once.

Does it work over fullscreen apps?

Yes. The plane floats above everything — even a fullscreen call or presentation — and it's click-through, so it never gets in your way. Then it flies off.

What's free, and what's Pro?

Free forever: the duck, one calendar, 5/10-minute reminders, your message and all five fonts. Pro ($4.99) adds 4 more characters with their own sounds, 4 plane colours, 4 banner themes, faster fly-bys, several calendars at once, and your own custom plane. The app is open source (MIT) on GitHub.

What about my privacy?

There's no backend. Your calendar is read straight from your device and kept only in memory — never written to disk or sent to us. Your sign-in is stored encrypted (Keychain / Windows DPAPI) only if you ask. No telemetry, no analytics. See the privacy note above.

Which platforms?

macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel) is available now — a Windows build (10 & 11) is coming soon. It lives in your menu bar and updates itself. The button above grabs the latest release.

How do I uninstall it?

Like any normal app: just drag it to the Trash on macOS (and once the Windows build lands, uninstall it from Settings). It leaves nothing behind on a server, because there's no server.
