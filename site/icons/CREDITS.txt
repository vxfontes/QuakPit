Icons bundled with the Quakpit landing page
===========================================

All icons in this folder are from OpenMoji — the open-source emoji and icon
project (https://openmoji.org).

License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

They are self-hosted here (not loaded from a CDN) so the site makes no
third-party requests, in line with Quakpit's zero-tracking principle.

Attribution is also shown in the site footer ("Icons by OpenMoji").
